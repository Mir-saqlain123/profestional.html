# 🏛️ CSC Center Kralnewah | Digital Seva

> A modern, responsive website for **CSC Center Kralnewah**, providing information about digital services, online applications, government services, financial assistance, education services, utility payments, and more.

🌐 **Live Website:** https://mirsaqlaincyber.tech

---

## 📌 About the Project

**CSC Center Kralnewah** is a responsive digital-service website designed to make it easier for local residents to discover and access available online services.

The website provides information about services such as:

- 🪪 Aadhaar-related assistance
- 💳 PAN Card services
- 📄 Domicile & Residence certificates
- 📑 Income & Category certificates
- 🗳️ Voter services
- 👷 e-Shram
- 🔐 DigiLocker assistance
- 🏦 Banking & financial services
- 💰 AEPS / DigiPay
- 🛡️ Insurance assistance
- 🎓 Scholarship applications
- 📝 Online forms & admissions
- ⚡ Electricity bill payments
- 💧 Water bill payments
- 📱 Mobile recharge & DTH
- ❤️ Digital health services
- 🌾 Agriculture-related services
- 🖨️ Printing, scanning & photocopy
- 📸 Photo services
- 🌐 Online application assistance
- 🚆 Railway & travel assistance
- ✈️ Bus / flight booking assistance

> **Note:** Actual service availability depends on authorization, eligibility, official portals, and the requirements of the concerned department or service provider.
